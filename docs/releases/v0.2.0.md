Everything from the web version is now in the app, so you can set up and run a budget entirely on your phone.

- **Accounts** (Settings): add your bank accounts, prepaid cards and cash, and tick what each one is for. Money moves, new bills and food money are routed by these.
- **Income and benefits** (Settings): paycheques, social assistance (ODSP, Ontario Works and more, with the deductions that work out the payment and report reminders), and government benefits like the CCB.
- **This month's income** (Month tab): change one month's amounts and dates, add a one-off payment, and lock or unlock a month.
- **Savings** (new tab): Emergency Savings and the Child Reserve against their targets, the Child Benefit split, savings funds (blank or ready-made, like Christmas or a vet fund), the Canada Learning Bond checklist, RDSP, RESP and TFSA estimates, the assistance asset limit, and the next 6 months.
- **Food**: both halves of the month, the food you've logged, and the food budget settings with a preview. Open it from Today, Month or Settings.
- **Balance checks**: type in what your accounts hold and see whether it matches the plan, and whether each account covers its bills until payday.
- Settings moved to the gear at the top right, to make room for the Savings tab.

Everything is saved the same way as the web version, so backups still move between the two unchanged.
