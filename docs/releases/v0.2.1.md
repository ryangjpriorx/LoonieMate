Updates now find you.

- LoonieMate checks for a new version in the background about twice a day, and whenever you open it.
- When there is one, you get a notification with a **Download** button. It opens an update screen where **Start update** downloads, checks and installs it in one go.
- If you leave the app while it downloads, it keeps going and lets you know when it's ready to install.
- On Android 13 and later, Today asks once whether you'd like these notifications. You can turn them off any time in Android's settings, or switch off "Check for updates automatically" in Settings.
