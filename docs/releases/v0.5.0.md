LoonieMate is easier to use for more people.

- **Settings → Accessibility** (only this phone):
  - **Bigger text**: Large, Larger or Largest, on top of your phone's own size.
  - **Bold text** and an **easier-to-read font** (Atkinson Hyperlegible, made for people with low vision).
  - **Stronger contrast**: darker text, outlined cards and stronger status colours.
  - **Colour-blind friendly colours**: blue for good and orange for problems instead of green and red.
  - **Less motion** (this is where Visual effects went). It also stays off whenever your phone's "Remove animations" is on.
  - **More time**: Undo and other messages stay until you close them.
  - **Bigger buttons**: larger buttons with more space between them.
- **Matched to your phone**: if your phone already uses large or bold text, more contrast, colour correction or TalkBack, LoonieMate offers the matching settings once, on Today.
- **Always on, for everyone**:
  - With TalkBack, each row is read as one sentence ("Bell, $95.00, due Wednesday, not paid"), with Mark paid, Mark done and Delete as actions.
  - Section titles are headings.
  - The status badge shows an icon as well as its colour.
  - Text is readable in every colour theme (some light-mode colours are slightly darker).
  - Screens still fit at your phone's largest text size.
- The web version has the same Accessibility settings, kept in that browser.
