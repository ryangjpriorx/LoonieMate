The first Android version of LoonieMate.

- Today: what to pay and which money to move today, food money for this half of the month, one-tap spending, debt payoff progress and the next few days.
- Month: the status of the month, help when it comes up short (give up a non-essential or pay a bill later), where each paycheck goes, and spending by category.
- Bills for adults, kids and pets, including debts with interest, missed payments and extra payments.
- Spending: everything logged this month, plus one-time expenses.
- Encrypted backups that work with the web version and between phones.
- Material You colours, and extra animation on phones with the power for it.
- Updates itself from GitHub.

Still in the web version for now: Food, Savings, Income and benefits, Accounts, and balance checks.
