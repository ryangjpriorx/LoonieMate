Getting on track now starts from what's really in your accounts.

- **Your balance is the truth.** Where you stand used to take this half's food money and Personal Expenses off again on top of your balance, as if that money were still waiting to be spent. Someone with $0 in the bank could be told they were hundreds short today. Now what's in your accounts pays the bills due before your next money comes in first, then food, then Personal Expenses, and anything the balance can't cover is counted as already spent.
- **"Food so far" is now a quick check.** Instead of guessing what you've spent, LoonieMate works out what's left: "After the bills due before Nov 27, about $75 is left for food until Nov 30. Sound right?" Tap **Yes, that's right** or **Change it**. The difference is logged once as "Spent before LoonieMate", so Month and Spending match what really happened.
- **A payday due today?** The balances step asks whether it's already in your balances. If it isn't yet (deposits often land later in the day), it's counted as coming in today.
- A $0 or low balance gets a kind word instead of an alarm: it just means what came in has gone on bills and spending, and LoonieMate starts from there.
- The steps are now: balances, this month so far, anything you're behind on, Personal Expenses, what's left for food, where you stand.
- **Web version:** the same maths.
