Personal Expenses: a little money for yourselves, planned in the open.

- **Everyone spends a little on themselves.** A coffee, smokes, a game, a night out. LoonieMate now plans for it instead of pretending it isn't there, and it never asks what it's for.
- **Set it up in Settings → Personal Expenses,** or in the new step of getting on track. One line per person (or one for everyone): how much, and per week, per paycheque or per month. Leave it blank for none.
- **It comes out after bills and food,** before what goes to savings. The Month screen and each payday's breakdown show it.
- **Today shows what's gone on it this month** next to what was planned. Logging it is up to you: pick **Personal Expenses** when you log spending. Not logging it is fine.
- **When money is short, it's only ever a suggestion.** Where you stand can offer to hold some back until the next payday (all of what's left, or half), and the status may mention it. Nothing is taken away unless you choose it, and it's back to normal next month.
- **Web version:** understands Personal Expenses, shows it in the month summary and lets you log it.
