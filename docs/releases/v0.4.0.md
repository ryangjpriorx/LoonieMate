Start from where you actually are.

Most people start a budget partway through a month: some money is spent, some bills are paid, maybe something is behind. LoonieMate now starts from today.

- **Five quick steps** (Today offers them once your accounts, income and bills are set up):
  1. What's in each account right now. This becomes your starting point.
  2. This month so far: tick the bills already paid, and see which paydays already came in.
  3. Anything you're behind on: push an unpaid bill to next month, or add something you owe and choose to pay it on a date, pay it off bit by bit (it stops once it's paid off), or decide later.
  4. Food so far: one rough number.
  5. **Where you stand**: every payday and bill until the next payday, the first day money runs short, and ways to cover it: pay part of a bill on payday, give up a non-essential this month, use Emergency Savings, and a reminder to call before the due date.
- **A starting month**: that month has no savings target. Today and Month show how it's going instead.
- **Get back on track** any time, in Settings → Need a fresh start?, after a missed paycheque or a surprise bill.
- The web version understands everything this saves.
