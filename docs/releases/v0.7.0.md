Forms that don't close by accident, and no more typing 0 into every box.

- **Every form is now a full page** instead of a panel that slides up from the bottom. A swipe or a stray tap can't close it any more, so nothing you typed gets lost.
- **Close it with the ✕ at the top, or your phone's back gesture.** If you've typed something, LoonieMate asks **Leave without saving?** first. Tap **Keep going** to carry on where you were.
- **Getting on track:** back goes to the previous step instead of closing. If you close it partway, it opens on the same step later that day.
- **Leave an account blank if it's empty.** When you check your balances, blank amounts count as $0.00. If any are blank, one question asks about all of them together, not one for each.
- **Turning the phone sideways** keeps the form and everything you've typed.
- **Web version:** clicking outside a form no longer closes it. The × and Esc ask first if you've typed something. Blank balances count as $0.00 after one question.
