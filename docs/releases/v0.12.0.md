A round of polish, and the settings the web version had that the app didn't.

- **Settings → Household and province.** Name your household, list the people who spend money (so **Who paid** works when you log spending), pick your **province or territory**, and change the plan dates and the baby's due date. The province matters: it sets the bank holidays that move paydays and bills, and which assistance programs and benefits you can add. Until now the app always used Ontario's.
- **Settings → Quick buttons:** see your one-tap spending buttons and remove the ones you don't use.
- **Spending tab → Last 6 months:** bills, food and everything else for each month, and the biggest change.
- **Spending tab → As a spreadsheet:** share your bills or everything logged as a CSV for Google Sheets or Excel.
- **Clear examples** straight from the note on the Month tab.
- **Reminders:** tapping Paid or Moved it on a notification now syncs to your other phone straight away, and reminders are handled in the background so they never slow the app down.
- **Safer with odd data:** a negative amount in an imported or synced budget counts as $0, and a damaged or tampered backup file can't freeze the app.
- **Easier to read at large text sizes:** a few cards and buttons no longer squeeze their words into a narrow column. Every form now has its title at the top.
- Closing "Make LoonieMate easier to use?" during setup carries on to the next step instead of skipping it.
- "Paycheque" everywhere, and a new budget is called LoonieMate until you name it.
- **Web version:** the same Household and province settings were already there; it now shows the LoonieMate loon in the header and no longer runs its start-up twice.
