Reminders for bills and money moves, built not to nag.

- **Only when something needs doing.** Nothing is sent on days with nothing due. One alert per reminder, which opens into a row for each thing.
- **Tick it off right there.** Each bill has a **Paid** button, each money move **Moved it**, and both have **In 1 hour** and **In 3 hours**. After Paid there's an **Undo**. The buttons ask you to unlock the phone first, and names and amounts stay off the lock screen.
- **Automatic payments** get a heads-up 1 or 2 days before they come out, so the money is in the right account. It moves earlier when it would land on a weekend or holiday.
- **Your times.** Settings → Reminders: once, twice or three times a day, at the times you pick (night owls too: a 1:00 a.m. follow-up still counts as the day before), different times on weekends, and a test notification.
- **Also in the first reminder, spaced out:** reporting income for ODSP or Ontario Works, tax season (Mondays), an old backup and the weekly balance check (Sundays).
- **If your phone delays them**, Today explains how to stop it putting LoonieMate to sleep.
- On Android 13 and later, Today asks once whether you'd like notifications.
