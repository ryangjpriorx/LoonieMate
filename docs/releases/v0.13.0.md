Employment Insurance, and clearer, more accurate social assistance.

- **Employment Insurance (EI)** is its own kind of income: paid every 2 weeks, with an optional **last payment** so nothing is planned after your benefits run out, and a **report reminder every two weeks**. Add it in Settings → Income and benefits.
- **Ontario Works estimates are now right.** OW counts pay from the 16th to the 15th and takes it off the next payment (pay from Oct 16 to Nov 15 lowers the December payment). Until now LoonieMate treated OW like ODSP, a month off.
- **No more "Which payment a month's pay reduces".** Each program's rule is built in and shown as a sentence with real months, like "Pay from September lowers the November payment".
- **No more deduction switches.** Each income says what kind it is (pay from a job, EI, or other income) and LoonieMate counts it the way the program does: EI in full, work pay with the right exemption, and one switch for someone on ODSP for a disability. Other income has a single "counts it" switch (spousal support yes, child support no).
- The payment estimate shows exactly which pay it used.
- **Web version:** the same forms and the same maths.
